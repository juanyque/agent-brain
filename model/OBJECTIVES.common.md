# OBJECTIVES (model-general)

Universal recurring objectives, shared by every brain through `_COMMON`. Each brain combines them with its local objectives in `WIP/OBJECTIVES.md` (the JOBS-style wrapper pattern): local objectives **extend** this set, never replace it.

## How to use

- Each brain's `WIP/OBJECTIVES.md` is the entry point: it points here and adds local objectives below.
- Daily evidence is recorded in the daily note under `# Actions` → `* [[OBJECTIVES]]:` as `* [[<objective name>]] — <short evidence>` (see `RULES-DAILY-NOTES.common.md` → Objectives tracking).
- At session or day close, the objectives review reads the active objectives (these plus the local ones) and records genuine evidence, passive or active. Never manufacture evidence.
- Do not create local notes named exactly like a canonical objective (e.g. a note called `Learn`): it would make Obsidian link resolution ambiguous.

## General objectives

- [[Learn]] — continuous learning
  - **Evidence**: exploring unfamiliar areas, debugging the unknown, technical reading, mastering new tools or domains.
- [[Support Others]] — helping others be effective (multiplier effect)
  - **Evidence**: pairing, mentoring, documented help, unblocking colleagues, messages or threads with other teams.
